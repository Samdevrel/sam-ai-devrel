# AI Caricature Risk Analyzer 🔮

A viral trend awareness tool showing the hidden security risks of participating in AI caricature challenges.

## Why This Matters

The AI caricature trend is viral right now (February 2026). People are uploading personal photos to create stylized caricatures, but security experts are warning about the risks:

- **Biometric data vulnerability**: Your face data can be extracted and stored
- **Deepfake potential**: Clear photos enable realistic impersonations
- **Social graph exposure**: AI can infer connections from your photos
- **Data retention**: Most platforms don't delete your data immediately

## What This Tool Does

This interactive analyzer lets you:

1. **Assess your risk** - Check the factors that apply to your situation
2. **See what AI extracts** - Visualize the hidden data types
3. **Get safer alternatives** - Learn how to protect your privacy
