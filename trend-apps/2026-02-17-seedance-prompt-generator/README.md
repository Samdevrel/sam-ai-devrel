## ✨ What is Seedance 2.0?

**Seedance 2.0** is ByteDance's revolutionary AI video generation model that went viral in February 2026. This powerful tool can process text, images, audio, and video simultaneously to create cinematic, professional-quality videos with just simple text prompts.

The trend exploded on Chinese social media, with users creating mind-bending videos featuring unlikely characters (like Kanye West and Kim Kardashian) starring in elaborate palace dramas speaking Mandarin. Elon Musk even commented "It's happening fast" on the breakthrough model.

## 🚀 Features

- **Genre Selection**: Choose from Sci-Fi, Fantasy, Drama, Comedy, Horror, Action, Romance, Thriller, or Musical
- **Setting Options**: Cyberpunk City, Ancient Palace, Space Station, Enchanted Forest, and more
- **Character Types**: Celebrity, Historical Figure, Anime Hero, Robot, Mythical Creature, etc.
- **Cinematic Prompts**: Generates detailed, professional video prompts with camera angles, visual effects, and technical specs
- **Random Inspiration**: One-click random prompt generation for instant creativity
- **Copy to Clipboard**: Easily copy prompts for use with AI video tools
- **Stunning UI**: Beautiful gradient backgrounds, floating particles, and smooth animations
