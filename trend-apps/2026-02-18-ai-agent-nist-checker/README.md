# AgentAudit — AI Agent NIST Compliance Checker

> Is your AI agent NIST-ready? Score yours in 60 seconds.

**Live:** https://2026-02-18-ai-agent-nist-checker.vercel.app

Built in response to the **NIST AI Agent Standards Initiative (CAISI)** launched February 18, 2026 — a government initiative to bring security, identity, and interoperability standards to autonomous AI agents.

## What It Does

Input your AI agent's configuration and get instant compliance scores across the three NIST CAISI pillars:

- 📋 **Standards Compliance** — Do you follow established agent protocols?
- 🔐 **Security & Identity** — Auth, rate limits, permission boundaries?
- 🌐 **Open Protocol Readiness** — Logging, auditability, open interoperability?

Get an overall grade (A–F), personalized recommendations, and a shareable report card.

## Why Now

NIST's CAISI just launched an AI Agent Standards Initiative focused on:
1. Industry-led development of agent standards
2. Community-led open source protocol development
3. Research in AI agent security and identity

The RFI on AI Agent Security is open until **March 9, 2026**. This tool helps developers self-assess before submission.
