Just open `index.html` in your browser - no installation needed!

Or visit the [live demo](https://ai-voice-podcast.vercel.app) →

## 💡 How It Works

1. Choose a celebrity voice style
2. Paste your podcast script
3. Click "Generate Podcast"
4. Listen to your AI-powered audio!

The app uses the **Web Speech Synthesis API** to generate speech with different voice characteristics (pitch, rate, timbre) to simulate celebrity voices.
