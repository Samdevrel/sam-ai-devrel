## 💡 Technical Deep Dive: ERC-7702 vs ERC-7710

### ERC-7702: Temporary Delegations
- **Purpose**: Short-term, revocable permissions
- **Use cases**: Testing, one-off actions, temporary access
- **Security**: Time-bound delegation reduces risk
- **Best for**: Experimentation, initial agent deployments

### ERC-7710: Smart Accounts for AI Agents
- **Purpose**: Persistent smart account infrastructure
- **Use cases**: Production AI agents, autonomous operations
- **Security**: Granular, revocable, intent-based
- **Best for**: Long-term agent deployments, complex workflows

### Key Differences:
| Feature | ERC-7702 | ERC-7710 |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Duration | Temporary | Persistent |
| Complexity | Lower | Higher |
| Use Case | Testing | Production |
| Security Model | Time-bound | Intent-based |
