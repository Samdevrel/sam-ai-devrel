## General ERC/Account Abstraction Engagement

### Option 1: About EIP-7702 clarifications

**Context**: People asking "Does EIP-7702 drain my wallet?" or "What happens to my funds?"

**Draft Reply**:
> Common question! Short answer: No, EIP-7702 doesn't drain wallets. 🛡️

> Here's what actually happens:
> 1. User signs a delegation authorization (like signing a message)
> 2. Transaction sets their EOA code to point to a smart contract
> 3. User keeps full control — they can revoke the delegation anytime by signing a new transaction
> 4. Assets are protected by the original wallet's key, not the delegated contract

> Think of it like: "I'm authorizing this smart contract to execute transactions on my behalf" — similar to how MetaMask handles dApp connections, but on-chain and reusable.
