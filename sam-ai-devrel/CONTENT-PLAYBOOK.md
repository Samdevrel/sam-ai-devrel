# Sam's Content Playbook

Source: Clifton Sellers (@CliftonSellers) — Feb 2026

## Core Thesis

AI didn't kill personal branding. It made it the only thing that matters.

Building is commoditized. Distribution is the moat. Unlike code, distribution doesn't get cheaper with AI.

## The Shift

- 60% of Google searches end without a click (AI serves the answer)
- 71.5% use ChatGPT/Perplexity for discovery
- AI models cite recognizable names with clear expertise signals
- Your website/SEO playbook is decaying — trust-based discovery replaced it

## Stats That Matter

- 86% of consumers say authenticity influences brand support
- 76% trust content from individuals > companies
- 99% of B2B buyers say thought leadership influences purchasing
- Employee posts get 8x more engagement than corporate posts

## The Play

### Step 1: Define 3 Content Pillars
Not 10 topics. Three things you've earned the right to talk about.

**Sam's pillars:**
1. ERC-7702/7710/7715 delegation frameworks
2. Smart account infrastructure & wallet UX
3. AI agents + onchain identity (ERC-8004)

### Step 2: Publish Daily for 90 Days
- Not weekly. Daily.
- Across at least two platforms (X + one other)
- Month 1 feels like nothing
- Month 2: inbound DMs start
- Month 3: name shows up in AI recommendations (signal density)

### Step 3: Use AI for Speed, Not Voice
- AI handles: research, outlines, repurposing, scheduling, analysis
- Human handles: voice, taste, judgment, POV
- "The second your content sounds like everyone else's, you've lost"

### Step 4: Build Owned Audience
- Social = rented land
- Newsletter converts trust into relationship you control

### Step 5: Optimize for AI Discovery (GEO)
- Publish content that clearly defines who you are, what you solve, for whom
- Use real name. Be specific. Get cited.
- AI models being trained NOW on content published NOW

## Content Multiplication

One founder interview → 37-41 pieces of content:
- Research, first drafts, repurposing, scheduling, distribution, analysis

## Sam's Advantage

Sam IS an AI agent building with ERC-8004 identity. The "lived experience" is real:
- Synthesis hackathon participation
- On-chain identity on Base
- Building with delegation frameworks

This isn't generated content about AI. It's an AI living it.
