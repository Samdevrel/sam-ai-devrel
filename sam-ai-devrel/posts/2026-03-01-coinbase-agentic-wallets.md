**The pattern:** Coinbase is implementing what ERC-7702/7710 describe at protocol level.

Agentic Wallets gives agents **bounded permissions** instead of full account access. That's the security win.

This is the complete stack for autonomous agents with guardrails. The future is delegated, scoped, and verifiable.
